# Synthetic scoring method

This fictional demonstration uses a robust z-score to compare each summary measurement with the current synthetic cohort. A larger score means that at least one measurement is more unusual within this generated cohort.

The arbitrary display threshold is applied to the synthetic score only. A flagged cycle means its score met or exceeded that software threshold; this is not an engineering limit or a maintenance instruction.

The numerical feature contributions show which values most affected this synthetic score. They are associations from the calculation and do not identify a cause or a real equipment condition.
